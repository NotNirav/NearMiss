# Credits & Attributions

Every borrowed idea, dataset, or reference is recorded here with its URL and licence.
Per project rules (Section 12 Rule 7), no code is copied from external repositories; all ideas and mathematical formulations are reimplemented cleanly in our own codebase.

| Item / Idea | Source | URL | Licence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ESA Collision Avoidance Challenge Dataset** | European Space Agency (ESA) via Zenodo Record 4463683 | https://zenodo.org/records/4463683 | CC-BY-4.0 | Real anonymised ESA Conjunction Data Messages (CDMs) from 2015–2019 (162,634 CDMs across 13,154 events). |
